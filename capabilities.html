Capabilities

Federal Supply Capabilities

FFS sources and delivers office, janitorial, paper, facility, and general industrial supplies for federal, state, and local government agencies and prime contractors. Our registered NAICS and PSC codes reflect our focus on nontechnical commodity supply.

Product Categories

Products We Source

FFS sources commonly purchased, nontechnical supplies through established distribution channels based on each solicitation's specifications, quantities, and delivery requirements.

Office & Administrative Supplies

Stationery, desktop essentials, organizational products, and everyday office consumables.

Janitorial & Cleaning Supplies

Brooms, brushes, mops, sponges, cleaners, and polishing products.

Paper & Facility Consumables

Paper products, restroom consumables, and other facility-use disposables.

General Industrial Supplies

General-purpose industrial and facility-support products sourced to match solicitation requirements.
