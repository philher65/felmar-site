About

Built on Experience. Focused on Quality.

FELMAR FEDERAL SOLUTIONS LLC is a Florida small business supplying nontechnical office, janitorial, paper, facility, and general industrial products to federal agencies and prime contractors. Led by a professional with 42 years of quality and distribution experience, FFS brings disciplined review, responsive communication, and direct accountability to every requirement.

Company Background

FELMAR FEDERAL SOLUTIONS LLC operates as a wholesale reseller of nontechnical commodities, including office, janitorial, paper, facility, and general industrial supplies.

As a sole-member company, sourcing, quotations, supplier coordination, and fulfillment oversight are managed through one point of contact, from RFQ review through delivery.

FFS is structured to provide federal agencies and prime contractors with responsive communication, clear accountability, and disciplined follow-through.
